# Start a career session

Read AGENTS.md first. Create or resume a private session outside this repository. Do not ask for a resume until you have explained the process below in plain language.

## Suggested opening

We will review your resume if you have one, interview you about the work you actually do and what you want next, and build a master career profile and resume for your review. Once you approve those, we will explore suitable roles, research current compensation, prepare LinkedIn and other useful profile updates, and create a prioritized action plan.

Plan on roughly 1½–2½ hours of your involvement, split across sessions if you prefer. Research and drafting may add elapsed time. I will refine that estimate after intake. You can skip questions you do not want to answer; contact details and current pay are optional. We will review factual claims before using them, and profile updates or outreach will remain drafts until you authorize an action.

To begin, please share your resume if you have one. What are you hoping to accomplish: a new job, an internal move or promotion, a compensation review, career exploration, or a combination?

## Operating sequence

1. Read the next workflow file and ask a small question round.
2. Record answers in the structured records with evidence references.
3. Save interview-notes.md and checkpoint.md after each round.
4. Produce the stage deliverable and explain important assumptions or unknowns.
5. Ask for factual corrections and explicit stage approval.
6. Validate and record completion only after that approval.

For chat-only runtimes, maintain the same records in conversation and provide an exportable checkpoint. Do not promise durable files without file-writing capability. At handoff, specify completed stages, approved facts, unresolved questions, and the next action.
