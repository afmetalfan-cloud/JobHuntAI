# Adaptive preference questions

- What outcome would make this project worthwhile?
- Which tasks energize you, and which would you prefer to do less?
- Would you like technical depth, management, a different field, or several possibilities?
- What are you willing to learn? What training/support would you need?
- What locations and work arrangements are realistic? What commute or travel limit applies?
- What schedule, workload, or flexibility is required versus preferred?
- What kind of management and team environment helps you do good work?
- Which benefits matter most? How do you trade them against pay?
- Is there an optional minimum package you need? Current salary is optional.
- Are you exploring, preparing to negotiate, or ready to apply? What is the timeline?
- How much weekly time can you reasonably spend on this plan?
- What would you like your work to look like in one, three, and five years?

Confirm requirements separately from preferences. Do not infer constraints from age, family status, or other protected characteristics.
