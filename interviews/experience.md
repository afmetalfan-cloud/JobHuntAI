# Adaptive experience questions

Ask 2–4 at a time, skip answered items, and explain why a follow-up matters.

## Role foundations

- What was your official title, employer, and approximate start/end date?
- Walk me through a typical week. What work is actually yours to complete?
- What does your job title fail to capture?
- Which responsibilities were added or changed?

## Scope and decision authority

- What decisions can you make without asking someone else?
- What errors, exceptions, incidents, or difficult cases come to you?
- Who relies on your work, and what happens if it is wrong or late?
- What scale can you verify: people, transactions, projects, budgets, systems, or frequency?

## Outcomes

- Tell me about a problem you solved. What did you do personally?
- What improved afterward, and how do you know?
- Do you have an exact metric, a reasonable estimate, or a qualitative result?
- What part was yours versus the team's?

## Tools and learning

- What tools do you use, and which tasks do you perform with them?
- What did you learn on the job? How long did it take to work independently?
- Which skills would transfer to a different employer or industry?
- What tools have you only observed or tried briefly?

## Leadership

- Have you trained, coached, coordinated, or improved others' work?
- Did you formally supervise people? Were hiring, performance reviews, schedules, or approvals yours?
- Describe influence without formal authority.

## Earlier experience

- Which earlier roles, projects, volunteer activities, or education add relevant skills?
- Which credentials are current, expired, or in progress?
- What important work have we not discussed?

Finish each round with a concise factual recap and corrections. Do not repeat the full resume verbatim.
