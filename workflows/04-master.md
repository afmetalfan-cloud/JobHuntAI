# Master profile and resume

Build a comprehensive master resume from confirmed roles and confirmed claims only. Preserve a structured profile for tailoring. Separate official titles from an optional scope description. Unknown dates stay unspecified; never turn them into invented dates. Include education and certifications only when confirmed, distinguishing current, expired, and in-progress credentials.

Draft a truthful summary, skill inventory, relevant work history, education, credentials, and selected projects/volunteer work. Do not send the master unchanged to every employer. Use `career.py render-resume` for a factual baseline, then edit for readability while preserving claims. The generator intentionally omits an AI-written summary.

List unresolved facts and estimates in open-questions.md. Have the participant review employment dates, titles, scope, metrics, credentials, and language. Obtain explicit approval before subsequent role/market work. Set profile.approved only after this approval. If making DOCX/PDF, inspect rendered pages and verify text order and legibility.

Completion: approved profile, master-resume.md, and open-questions.md with either specific unresolved items or an explicit statement that none remain.
