# Career preferences

Use interviews/preferences.md. Ask about enjoyable work, draining tasks, desired learning, leadership interest, stability, work environment, schedule, travel, location/commute, remote/hybrid expectations, compensation priorities, benefits, timing, and longer-term direction.

Separate hard requirements, flexible preferences, and unknowns. Ask whether a difficult constraint can change; do not silently relax it. Avoid interpreting a current role or a missing credential as a permanent career limit. Record what the participant is willing to learn and evidence of prior learning.

Store preferences.json and career-criteria.md. A financial minimum is optional and is not a market benchmark. Do not collect protected characteristics to infer career suitability. Completion: preferences confirmed, target market and currency recorded, and priorities resolved or explicitly left unknown.
