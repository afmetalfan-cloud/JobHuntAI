# Optional modules

## Tailored applications

Request a current job posting. Verify essential requirements, constraints, and active status when possible. Map requirements to confirmed claims; draft a targeted resume and optional cover letter. Retain truthful official titles and dates. Do not inflate proficiency or remove essential nuance. Explain unmet requirements. Obtain separate authorization before submitting.

## Recruiters and networking

Define role/geography criteria. Verify recruiter identities and current relevance using available sources. Draft personal messages within current platform limits. Do not invent familiarity or send without authorization. Record source, rationale, draft, and follow-up status.

## Application tracking

Use templates/application-tracker.csv privately. Record actual application status only when confirmed. Track role, source URL, pay, constraints, dates, next action, and tailored documents. Do not put tracking data in GitHub issues.

## Interview preparation

Build examples from confirmed experience. Prepare honest responses for gaps and questions testing management quality, workload, training, flexibility, and benefits. Avoid memorized fabricated stories. Capture new verified information back into the profile.

## Compensation conversation and offers

Draft language suited to the person's voice and goal. Use evidence-backed current-scope or expanded-scope arguments. Compare base, variable pay, equity, retirement, PTO, insurance, commute, schedule, development, and risk separately. Mark unknown offer terms. Negotiation actions require authorization.

## Follow-up

Update plans when circumstances change and refresh dated research before reuse. Scheduling recurring work requires a separately authorized automation and an available scheduler. This repository does not provide background execution.
