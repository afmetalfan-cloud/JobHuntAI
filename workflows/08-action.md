# Career action plan

Create a realistic plan matched to the goal. A compensation-review participant may prioritize scope documentation and a manager conversation; an active job seeker may prioritize target employers, networking, applications, and interview preparation. Do not require applications for an exploratory participant.

career-action-plan.md includes next 7/30/90 days, manageable weekly effort, owners, dependencies, success measures, compensation conversation plan, skills development, and 1/3/5-year hypotheses. Longer-term paths are scenarios, not predictions. Compare opportunities against requirements and total package, not base pay alone.

Use workflows/optional-modules.md for tailored resumes, recruiter drafts, tracking, interviews, and offers. Core completion includes a usable handoff checkpoint and outstanding items; it does not imply a job, raise, or account update occurred. Completion: participant approves priorities, validation passes, and all prior stages are complete.
