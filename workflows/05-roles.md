# Role exploration

Explore direct, adjacent, and stretch role families. Use actual duties and demonstrated learning, not just title keywords. Explain transferable skills and why each role could fit. Distinguish trainable tool/process gaps from essential credentials or responsibilities that cannot be assumed.

For every proposed role, record category, linked claim IDs, gaps, learning plan, requirement conflicts, and rationale in roles.json and role-map.md. Include multiple industries where reasonable. A match percentage may be descriptive only; never use it as an automatic rejection threshold. If no plausible stretch option exists, explain the evidence and alternative development paths.

Do not label unsupported management or accounting qualifications as demonstrated. Do not exclude a role solely because the participant has not previously held its title. Completion: all three categories considered, fit and constraints discussed, and participant approves which families should be researched.
