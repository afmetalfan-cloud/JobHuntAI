# Current market and compensation

Requires approved master profile and selected role families. Read docs/research-method.md. Research live employer-disclosed ranges and relevant government occupational data, supplemented by transparent compensation surveys. Check publication/update dates and access dates; record local versus remote-market applicability.

Store research.json and compensation-report.md. Each recommendation references specific evidence, explains duty overlap and differences, and reports low/target/high annual base compensation in one stated currency, with assumptions and confidence. Separate current_scope, external_move, and stretch recommendations; label unsupported lanes as unavailable, not as invented numbers.

If browsing is unavailable or evidence too weak, set status=blocked with reasons and a research plan. Do not complete this stage. The participant can resume with better evidence. Fictional reference data is never valid real-market evidence.

Distinguish a negotiation request from market value, and a participant's acceptable minimum from either. Compare total reward separately; do not add benefits into base salary. Completion: sufficient dated relevant evidence, limitations explained, participant understands and approves the report.
