# Profile positioning

Use the approved master and chosen role map. Draft LinkedIn headline, About, experience, skills-to-role mapping, education/credentials, and job preferences. Preserve the person's voice and verified scope. Recommend visibility settings based on their preferences; never assume they want public Open to Work.

Recommend additional websites based on occupation, geography, and goals: general job boards, specialist boards, recruiter platforms, portfolios, or professional associations as appropriate. Verify current platform features before giving feature-specific instructions. Do not claim paid subscriptions are required without evidence.

profile-update-plan.md contains ready-to-use copy, prioritized websites with reasons, changes needing participant input, and a verification checklist. Account writes are a distinct authorization step; drafts alone do not authorize publication. Completion: participant approves copy and platform priorities. Actual account updates may remain manual and are tracked separately.
