# Experience interview

Use interviews/experience.md adaptively. Cover relevant paid, volunteer, project, military, caregiving-derived transferable experience when the participant wants it included, and career breaks without demanding explanations. Do not assume dates, credentials, or seniority.

For each role, discover recurring work, decisions, complexity, independence, systems, scale, accomplishments, failure recovery, training, and leadership. Ask what changed because of their work. Ask follow-ups to distinguish participation from ownership and oversight from formal management. Distinguish tools used from proficiency level.

Store roles and claims in profile.json. Each role and claim references a private evidence source; confirmations are explicit. Separate estimated metrics from exact ones. If no quantitative evidence exists, use accurate qualitative outcomes. Save interview-notes.md after each round with answers, confirmations, unknowns, and next questions.

Completion: experience reviewed by participant, at least one role and one confirmed claim recorded, unresolved details retained. No-resume participants receive the same interview quality.
