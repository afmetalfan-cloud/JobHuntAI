# Compensation research standard

## Scope before numbers

Define duties, seniority, industry, country/region, currency, work arrangement, and the role families. Research current work separately from expanded responsibilities. The person's desired salary is not evidence of market value.

## Evidence

Prioritize relevant employer-disclosed salary ranges and official occupational statistics. Use transparent recruiter surveys and salary datasets as corroboration, noting sample size, methodology, year, location, and whether figures describe starting pay or incumbents. Prefer original employer postings over copied aggregations. Relevance matters more than collecting many sources.

For each record retain: title, URL, publisher, accessed_on, published_on (null when unknown), country/region, currency, pay basis, low/high, whether it was live/verified, duty overlap, missing duties, and limitations. Current postings should be checked at access time; archived postings must be labeled historical. Do not infer an unknown publication date from access date.

Aim for at least three relevant independent publishers per researched market/role cluster. When evidence is sparse, say so. The helper requires at least two publishers and documented rationale for its structural completion gate; this minimum is not a guarantee of research quality. Recheck evidence older than 30 days before finalizing or reusing a report. If a reference statistic is older but still useful, retain its actual year and explicitly refresh its access check.

## Normalize and compare

Keep currencies separate unless conversion is sourced and dated. Annualize hourly pay only with stated hours/week and weeks/year; do not assume every job is 40 hours and 52 paid weeks. Distinguish base from bonus, commission, equity, and total compensation. Broad occupation averages are context, not direct pricing of a specific person.

Map duties to each comparator. Discount missing essential scope; do not add overlapping role salaries or average dissimilar jobs. Show ranges with confidence and limitations. An attractive high-end posting is not proof that the participant will obtain that pay.

## Recommendations

Provide three lanes: current_scope, external_move, stretch. Every supported lane lists role family, annual base low/target/high, currency, market, evidence_ids, confidence, rationale, and gaps. When unsupported, use status=unavailable and a reason. Report a practical negotiation target separately from a financial minimum and distinguish current responsibilities from a proposed expanded role.

The report should include source comparisons, normalization assumptions, lower/upper-bound reasoning, package considerations, next evidence to gather, and retrieval date. Use accessible links near claims. Do not describe a supported range as an entitlement or promise.

## Missing capabilities

Without browsing, use status=blocked, list blocked_reasons, and write a concrete research plan. Without sufficient comparable evidence, do the same. No current pay recommendation should be generated from memory or examples.
